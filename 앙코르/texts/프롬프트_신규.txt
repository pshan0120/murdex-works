[앙코르(신규) - 이미지 생성 프롬프트 — 1단계: 화풍 3종 공통 앵커]

리와인드용 `프롬프트.txt`의 "동화책 수채화풍" 확정 프롬프트를 기준으로, 새 앙코르의 3단계
화풍(색연필 손그림풍 → 동화책 수채화풍 → 파스텔 플랫 벡터풍, 정방향 매칭)에 맞게 확장한다.
캐릭터 정체성(비례, 색 패턴, 얼굴·귀 생김새)은 세 화풍 내내 고정 — 화풍 전환이 "다른 개"로
보이면 안 된다는 원래 원칙을 그대로 유지.

====================================================================
0. 공통 캐릭터 정체성 앵커 (강아지, 세 화풍 모두 동일하게 포함)

a small Yorkshire Terrier dog character, compact toy-breed body proportions, erect
V-shaped ears, round dark eyes, small black nose, slightly elongated snout, small
pink tongue sticking out, small white patch on the chest, short docked tail,
sitting pose, three-quarter front view, warm and sincere mood, not exaggeratedly
cute or comedic

공통 Negative prompt (모든 프롬프트 동일):
realistic, photorealistic, photo, 3D render, other dog breed, chibi, kawaii
exaggerated, humanoid, text, watermark

====================================================================
1. 화풍별 문구 (0번 앵커 뒤에 이어 붙이고, 그 뒤에 3장의 나이별 문구를 붙인다)

--- 색연필 손그림풍 (1~3살, 서투른 시기) ---
colored pencil illustration style, visible pencil strokes and cross-hatching
texture, slightly rough and hand-drawn linework, soft grainy paper texture, warm
earthy color palette (cream, ochre, muted brown, dusty rose), textured uneven
coloring within outlines, warm nostalgic children's picture book aesthetic,
solid pure white background, isolated on white, no background elements, gentle
diffused lighting, square 1:1 aspect ratio, centered composition

--- 동화책 수채화풍 (4~11살, 성숙기) — 리와인드 확정본 그대로 재사용 ---
soft watercolor illustration style, gentle color bleeding and blending at the
edges, muted pastel palette (cream, dusty pink, soft blue), thin soft brown
outline, warm nostalgic children's picture book aesthetic, solid pure white
background, isolated on white, no background elements, gentle diffused
lighting, square 1:1 aspect ratio, centered composition

--- 파스텔 플랫 벡터풍 (12~16살, 노년기) ---
flat vector illustration style, soft pastel color palette (powder blue, blush
pink, cream, sage green), clean smooth flat shapes with minimal shading, soft
rounded edges, subtle gradient shading only where needed, gentle muted
lighting, warm nostalgic picture book aesthetic, solid pure white background,
isolated on white, no background elements, square 1:1 aspect ratio, centered
composition

[경계 구간 안내] 3→4살, 11→12살 전환 시 급격한 화풍 변화가 어색하면, 그 경계에 걸친
단서 한두 개는 두 화풍을 절반씩 섞은 프롬프트(예: "loosely blending colored pencil
texture into soft watercolor bleeding")로 점이적 전환을 시도해볼 것 — 기존 원칙 유지.

2. 나이별 신체·색상 문구 (1번 화풍 문구 뒤에 이어 붙임)

--- 1살 (아기, 성견의 약 1/2 크기) — 색연필 손그림풍과 조합 ---
a very young Yorkshire Terrier puppy character, very small baby puppy body at
half the adult body size, oversized head-to-body ratio typical of an adorable
baby puppy, medium-length slightly shaggy soft puppy fur, smooth rounded baby
contours, soft chubby puppy cheeks, big glossy round innocent eyes, round puppy
tummy, almost entirely black puppy coat with minimal tan accents on legs and
cheeks, no silver/grey fur yet

--- 2살 (성견의 약 2/3 크기) — 색연필 손그림풍과 조합 ---
a small juvenile Yorkshire Terrier dog character, compact young dog proportions
at two-thirds the adult body size, visibly older than a baby puppy but
definitely younger than an adult dog, medium-length slightly shaggy fur, gentle
juvenile face shape with soft cheeks, mostly black coat over the body and dark
head with minimal golden tan accents on cheeks and legs, no white/grey face fur
yet

--- 3살 (성견 크기로 막 자람, 색연필 마지막 해) — 색연필 손그림풍과 조합 ---
a young adult Yorkshire Terrier dog character, now at full compact toy-breed
adult body proportions, medium-length slightly shaggy fur, coat pattern just
settling into the classic silvery-grey and black back fading to tan/golden-
brown on the legs and face, small hints of silver just beginning to show, still
mostly youthful dark coloring overall

--- 4~11살 (성견, 안정기) — 동화책 수채화풍과 조합 ---
a small Yorkshire Terrier dog character (adult), medium-length slightly shaggy
fur, natural grown out coat, silvery-grey and black coat on the back fading to
tan/golden-brown on the legs and face, healthy and settled adult coloring

--- 12~16살 (노년기) — 파스텔 플랫 벡터풍과 조합 ---
a small elderly Yorkshire Terrier dog character, adult toy-breed body
proportions, medium-length fur with the same silvery-grey and tan adult
coloring as before, but with localized white frosting specifically around the
muzzle and chin (a common sign of old age in dogs regardless of breed), eyes
slightly cloudy with a faint bluish-grey haze suggesting early cataracts, a
slightly slower and more settled posture with relaxed shoulders, gentle tired
but warm expression, still clearly the same dog underneath the visible signs
of old age
