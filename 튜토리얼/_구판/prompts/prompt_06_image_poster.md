### [옵션 1: 으스스한 외경]

Heavy rain is pouring down, creating reflections on the wet asphalt. The store's neon sign "24h HAPPY MART" flickers ominously. Through the glass window, a silhouette of a person can be seen standing near the counter, looking tense. The atmosphere is cold, mysterious, and thrilling.
