### 1. 편의점 전체 전경 (내부)
**용도**: 게임 메인 화면 / 조사 단계 배경
**Prompt:**
Wide interior shot of a modern convenience store in Korea. Shelves are stocked with colorful snacks and drinks. The lighting is bright fluorescent ceiling lights but slightly eerie. Large glass windows at the front show heavy rain pouring outside in the darkness. The floor is tiled and shiny. No people in the shot. Hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, architectural photography.

### 2. 카운터 & 금고
**용도**: 조사 장소 (카운터)
**Prompt:**
Close-up view of a convenience store counter. There is a POS machine, a scanner, and some impulse buy items like gum and candy. Below the counter, a small safe door is wide open and empty. A feeling of a crime scene. Detailed texture, realistic lighting.

### 3. 창고 (사건 현장)
**용도**: 조사 장소 (창고)
**Prompt:**
Interior of a convenience store storage room. It is cluttered with cardboard boxes stacked high. The lighting is dim, coming from a single bulb. In the center of the concrete floor, there is a dark ominous stain (implying blood, but not too gory). A metal mop bucket and a broken mop handle are in the corner. Scary and claustrophobic atmosphere. Horror movie style.

### 4. 화장실 복도
**용도**: 조사 장소 (복도)
**Prompt:**
A narrow, dimly lit hallway leading to a restroom. The walls are painted a dull beige. At the end of the hall, there is a door with a restroom sign. To the side, there is another door labeled "Staff Only". The floor is wet with muddy footprints. Eerie atmosphere.
