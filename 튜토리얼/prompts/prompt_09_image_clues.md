# 머더 미스테리 이미지 생성 프롬프트: 단서 (Clues)

## 도구: Gemini (NanoBanana)
게임 내에서 획득하는 아이템(단서) 카드에 들어갈 이미지를 생성합니다.
모든 이미지는 **흰색 배경(또는 투명 배경)** 위주로 깔끔하게 생성되거나, **어두운 바닥에 놓인 사물** 형태로 통일감을 주어야 합니다. 
(추천: 어두운 바닥에 스포트라이트를 받는 느낌)

---

### 1. 시체 (점장) 
* **주의**: 너무 잔인하지 않게.
**Prompt:**
A pair of men's shoes and legs lying on a concrete floor, seen from a low angle. The upper body is obscured by shadows or boxes. A pool of dark liquid is spreading on the floor near the head area. Implied crime scene. Cinematic lighting, dramatic shadows.

### 2. CCTV 화면
**Prompt:**
A grainy security camera monitor screen displaying a black and white view of a convenience store aisle. On the screen, a blurry figure in a raincoat is seen walking away. Digital glitch effects on the screen. technological texture.

### 3. 쇠파이프 (흉기)
**Prompt:**
A close-up of a metal pipe lying on a dirty floor. It looks like a part of a shelf. One end of the pipe is stained with a dark red substance. Sharp focus, high detail, realistic texture.

### 4. 외상 장부
**Prompt:**
An old, worn-out notebook lying open on a table. The pages are filled with handwritten names and numbers. One name is circled in red marker. A ballpoint pen is lying next to it. Top-down view.

### 5. 독촉장 (핸드폰 문자)
**Prompt:**
A close-up of a smartphone screen glowing in the dark. The screen displays a threatening text message. The background is blurred darkness. Focus on the phone screen.

### 6. 헬멧
**Prompt:**
A black motorcycle helmet sitting on a table. It is wet with raindrops. The visor is dark and reflective. A small red smudge is visible on the chin strap. Cinematic lighting.

### 7. 빈 소주병
**Prompt:**
An empty green soju bottle rolling on the floor. It looks discarded. The label is partially torn. Realistic glass texture.
